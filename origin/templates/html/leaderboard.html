    How Ranking Works

    Your rank is based on your total streak sum across all active commitments. The higher your combined streaks, the higher you climb. You must opt in via profile settings to appear.

    Private Scores

    If a user's streak shows , they chose to hide their count. Their rank still counts — the number is simply not displayed.

    Zeal vs Streak

    This leaderboard ranks by total streak sum. Your personal Zeal Score weighs commitment count × average streak — a different metric that measures overall discipline intensity.
