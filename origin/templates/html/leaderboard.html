How It Works

Earned, not gamed.

How scores are calculated

Your leaderboard score is the sum of all active commitment streaks you have running simultaneously. One 30-day streak = 30 points. Three commitments each at 10 days = 30 points. The system rewards breadth of discipline, not just one habit you've been riding.

Privacy is the default

Your score is hidden from this board unless you explicitly opt in from your profile settings. When you do appear, only your public username is shown — never your real name, email, or the content of your journal entries. You can opt out any time.

Weekly reset, permanent record

The board resets every Monday at midnight. Previous weeks are archived and viewable via the week switcher above. Consistent top-10 finishes will eventually appear on a Hall of Fame page — coming soon.

Zeal Score vs Streak Score

Your Zeal Score (shown on your dashboard) is a personal discipline index — it includes streak quality and consistency rate. The leaderboard uses a simpler raw combined streak total so the ranking is objective and comparable across all users.
