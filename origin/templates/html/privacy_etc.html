Help Center

How do I start my first commitment?

After signing up, you'll complete a quick onboarding where you define your commitment, why it matters, your minimum effort, and your goal. Then check in daily — one honest sentence is all it takes.

How do I create an account?

Click Start Free on the landing page. You can sign up with email, Google, or Facebook. No credit card required.

I can't log into my account

Use the Forgot Password link on the login page. If you used Google or Facebook to sign up, make sure you're using the same social login method. Contact support if you're still stuck.

Can I change my username?

Your public User ID (used for finding partners) can be changed from your Profile settings. Your account email cannot be changed directly — contact support if needed.

How can I delete my account and data?

Go to Account Settings → Profile → Delete Account for self-service deletion. You can also email or WhatsApp us. See our Privacy Policy (Section 8) for full details.

Who can see my journal entries?

Only you. Your journal entries, reasons, and mood data are completely private. If you opt into the leaderboard, only your streak count and public User ID are visible — never your entries.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Export all your commitments, entries, and analytics in JSON or CSV format from Account Settings → Export Data.

Do you use tracking cookies?

No. We only use essential cookies for authentication (keeping you signed in) and theme preference. Zero tracking. Zero advertising cookies.

How do I cancel my Pro subscription?

Go to Account Settings → Billing → Cancel Subscription. Your Pro features remain active until the end of your current billing period.

Can I get a refund?

First‑time Pro subscribers are covered by a 7‑day money‑back guarantee. Contact support within 7 days of your initial payment.

What's the difference between plans?

Starter (Free): 5 active commitments, daily check-in reminder via email, weekly report summary.

Pro (₦1,000/month): 50 active commitments, daily check-in reminders via WhatsApp, email or push notifications, advanced weekly report summary, personalized coach, 1 accountability partner.

Team (₦2,000/month): Unlimited commitments, all reminder channels including offline reminders, custom periodical summaries, personalized coach, 4 accountability partners, advanced analytics with actionable recommendations, and the ability to create Groups.

What happens if I miss a day?

Your streak resets to zero. The app will ask you to reflect honestly on why you missed. This isn't punishment — it's accountability. Every day is a new chance to start again.

How do accountability partners work?

Search for a user by their public User ID. Send a partner request. If they accept, you'll both see each other's consistency scores — never journal entries. You can remove partners anytime.

How do I set up reminders?

During onboarding or from Account Settings → Reminders, you can set a daily reminder time and choose between email or WhatsApp delivery.

Can I use the app without appearing on leaderboards?

Yes. Leaderboards are completely optional. You can disable them during onboarding or anytime from Account Settings → Privacy.

What is the Zeal Score?

Zeal Score is your overall discipline index — calculated from your active commitments and their average streak length. Unlike a single streak, it measures intensity across everything you're tracking. More commitments with longer streaks = higher Zeal.
