            How this report is calculated

            • Days you checked in, divided by days elapsed so far in the period. Today only counts once it has ended in your local timezone.

            • Consecutive days with at least one completed check-in on an active commitment, counting backward from your most recent entry.

            • The single mood you pick when you check in — nothing inferred from your writing. We never run sentiment analysis on your private notes.

            • Plain word count on your entry text. "This week vs last week" is a simple comparison against your own history — never against other users.
